The least bit is a trench.

Cicada hid payloads with OutGuess and with simpler LSB. We do not ship OutGuess. We ship the habit: take the last bit of each red sample, stop at a null, read UTF-8. The workbench will do it. So will a script of your own.

Specimen: /media/wing.png. Also try a strings pass. Files sometimes carry a tail after their declared end. The LSB bench will paint a bit-plane if you want to see the trench.
